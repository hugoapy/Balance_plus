Privacy Policy for Balance+
Effective Date: November 6, 2026

Thank you for using Balance+. Your privacy is our top priority. This Privacy Policy explains the nature of the information within the application. Our core principle is that your data belongs to you and never leaves your device. Balance+ is designed to be a completely private space.

1. Information We Store and Do Not Collect
Balance+ operates entirely locally on your device and does not collect any user information.

We DO NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal or financial information to servers, as we do not operate server infrastructure for this application.

The information handled by the application is stored exclusively on your device, divided into the following local data categories:

a) User-Generated Data (Stored Locally Only)
Transaction Data: Any income or expense record you create, including amount, date, category, description, and type of entry (income/expense), is stored solely in your device's local database.

Account and Budget Data: Information regarding financial accounts you configure (e.g., "Cash", "Bank Account") and budget limits you establish for categories is saved locally on your device.

b) Application Configuration Data (Stored Locally Only)
Currency and Display Preferences: Default currency, date formatting, theme (light/dark), and other presentation settings are stored locally to remember your preferences.

Biometric Lock Settings: Your choice to enable or disable fingerprint/Face ID lock is stored securely on your device.

c) In-App Purchases Data (Managed via Google Play)
PRO Features Purchase: Purchases of PRO Features are processed directly and securely by Google Play Billing. We do not store, access, or collect your credit card details, bank account info, or billing address. Google handles all purchase validation and license restoration associated with your Google account.

2. How We Use Your Information
The information generated and stored on your device is used solely within the application for the following purposes:

To provide core functionality: Calculating your balance, generating income/expense reports, and displaying your financial summaries.

To personalize your experience: Applying your selected currency formatting and UI theme.

To manage local alerts: The app may use budget limits or recurring transaction dates to schedule local notifications on your device. These notifications are handled strictly by your phone's operating system and do not send any information to us.

All your data is processed and stored exclusively on your device. We have no access to your data.

3. Data Storage and Security
All information is saved within the app’s secure, private storage managed by your operating system.

The "Export Data" feature allows you to create a backup of your data into a file. This file is generated and remains on your device under your complete control.

4. Information Sharing
We do not share, sell, or rent your information to third parties, as we do not collect it in the first place.

5. Children's Privacy
Our application is not directed to children under the age of 13. Since we do not collect data, we do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.

6. Changes to this Privacy Policy
Because Balance+ operates locally on your device without server communication or user tracking, any updates to this Privacy Policy will take effect immediately upon being published in an application update on the Google Play Store or hosted documentation repository.
